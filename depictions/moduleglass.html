Version 1.1.18

This release keeps the complete Module Glass settings and validated renderer, removes prerelease labeling, and adds working device activation.

The activation controls now appear at the very end of the main settings page—after every customization control—using the same proven pattern as NextLock.

Simple lifetime activation

Settings shows your activation status and generated Device ID, with buttons to copy the ID, buy or activate the $1 USD lifetime license, and check activation against the live Next Solution registry.

Compatibility

For iOS 16 and newer. Separate packages are provided for standard rootless and RootHide environments; install only the package that matches your jailbreak, then respring from Module Glass settings.
