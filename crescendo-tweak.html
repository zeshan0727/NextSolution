Crescendo 1.0 Adds a Volume Slider to the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

Crescendo is a version 1.0 package by yves in the Lock Screen category. Its description says that it adds a volume slider to the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island. The metadata lists iphoneos-arm and iphoneos-arm64 package variants, plus dependencies including ElleKit or MobileSubstrate at version 0.9.5000 or newer, firmware 16.0 or newer, and PreferenceLoader.
