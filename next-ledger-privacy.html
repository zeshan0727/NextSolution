Next Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Next Ledger is a personal ledger, expense tracking, budgeting, reporting, and backup utility for iPhone. This policy describes how the App Store version of Next Ledger handles information.

Data stored in Next Ledger

Financial records that you enter or import, such as transactions, account names, categories, notes, budgets, vendor rules, and related settings, are stored on your device. Next Ledger does not connect to your bank account and does not perform banking, payments, lending, investing, trading, or other regulated financial services.

No SMS or Messages access

The App Store version of Next Ledger does not read, capture, monitor, or access SMS, iMessage, or the Messages database. Any separate jailbreak or sideload-only tools distributed outside the App Store are not part of the App Store version covered by this policy.

No third-party AI in the App Store version

The App Store version does not include OpenAI, DeepSeek, or another third-party AI chat or AI recommendation service, and it does not send ledger information to an AI provider.

Backups and files

If you enable iCloud Drive Sync, Next Ledger may store a backup in your private iCloud Drive container associated with your Apple Account. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

You may also choose to export or import CSV or JSON files using Apple’s system file picker. If you choose a third-party file provider such as Google Drive in the Files picker, that provider handles the selected file according to its own privacy terms. Next Ledger does not automatically access files outside the locations you select.

Notifications and Shortcuts

Next Ledger may use local notifications for user-configured budgeting reminders or alerts. Supported Apple Shortcuts actions operate only when you configure or run them through Apple’s Shortcuts system.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The App Store version of Next Ledger does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier to track you.

Data sharing

Next Solution does not sell your Next Ledger data. The app does not transmit your ledger records to Next Solution servers. Data leaves the app only when you intentionally use a system feature such as iCloud backup, file export, or a file provider you select.

Data deletion

You can delete records inside Next Ledger or remove the app and its local data from your iPhone. Files or backups you intentionally placed in iCloud Drive or another file provider can be deleted from that service using its normal file-management tools.

Children

Next Ledger is a general personal finance utility and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if Next Ledger’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
