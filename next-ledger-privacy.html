Next Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Next Ledger is a personal ledger, expense tracking, budgeting, reporting, and backup utility for iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information you enter

Financial records that you enter or import, including transactions, account names, categories, notes, budgets, vendor rules, and related settings, are stored on your device. Next Ledger does not connect directly to financial institutions and does not process payments.

iCloud backup

If you enable iCloud Drive Sync, Next Ledger may store a backup in the app's private iCloud container associated with your Apple Account. iCloud is provided by Apple and is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Import and export

You may choose to export or import CSV or JSON files using Apple's system file picker. If you select a third-party file provider in the Files interface, that provider handles the file according to its own terms and privacy practices. Next Ledger only accesses files and locations you explicitly select.

Notifications and Shortcuts

Next Ledger may use local notifications for user-configured budget alerts. Supported Apple Shortcuts actions run only when you configure or invoke them through Apple's Shortcuts system.

Data collection and tracking

Next Solution does not receive or collect your ledger records through the app. Next Ledger does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use cross-app tracking.

Data sharing

Next Solution does not sell your Next Ledger data. Data leaves the app only when you intentionally use a system feature such as iCloud backup, file export, or a file provider you select.

Data deletion

You can delete records inside Next Ledger or remove the app and its local data from your iPhone. Files or backups you intentionally placed in iCloud Drive or another file provider can be deleted using that service's normal file-management tools.

Children

Next Ledger is a general personal finance utility and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if Next Ledger's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
