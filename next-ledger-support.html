Next Ledger Support

Next Ledger is a personal ledger and budgeting utility for iPhone. It helps you manually record income, expenses and transfers, organize accounts and categories, review reports, set budgets, and create backups.

Getting started

  • Create one or more accounts and choose their currencies.
  • Add income, expenses, or transfers from the Home or Transactions screens.
  • Use Reports to review your recorded activity.
  • Use Settings to export CSV/JSON backups or enable iCloud Drive backup.
  • Use Apple Shortcuts if you want to create your own personal data-entry automations.

Privacy

The App Store version does not read SMS or iMessage data and does not include third-party AI, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, bank-account linking, payments, investing, lending, or trading services.
