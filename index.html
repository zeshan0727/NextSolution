Frequently asked questions

Read these points before making changes to your device.

Is jailbreaking completely risk-free?

No. Modifying system software can cause instability, security issues, data loss, or compatibility problems. Back up your device and use a guide confirmed for your exact model and iOS version.

Can I remove a jailbreak later?

Many jailbreaks include a restore or remove option. A full Finder or iTunes restore may also return the device to stock iOS, depending on the tool and version.

Are the tutorials free?

Yes. The written guides and videos provided by Next Solution are free.

Why is a tweak not working?

Check the supported iOS version, jailbreak type, architecture, dependencies, and whether the package was built for rootless or rootful environments.
