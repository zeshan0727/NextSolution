PodsGrant 0.5.4: AirPods Compatibility Tweak for Older Devices

PodsGrant is a system and utilities tweak by Ruphane. Its package description says it provides better AirPods compatibility on old devices. Version 0.5.4 is listed for the iphoneos-arm architecture and records MobileSubstrate, a firmware requirement of 13.0 or later, and PreferenceLoader as dependencies. The supplied facts do not confirm a specific operating-system release, device model, AirPods model, installation result, or performance outcome.
