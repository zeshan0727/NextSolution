Phonehub Adds Caller ID, Auto Redial and Smarter Call Controls to iPhone

Phonehub is a broad Phone app upgrade rather than one small call tweak. It combines caller identification, redial behavior, SIM selection and in-call convenience features in one package.

Version1.1.3
Listed supportiOS 15.0–17.0
Price$3.99
SourceHavoc

What Phonehub actually changes

The package's headline feature is caller identification using multiple lookup sources. The developer also includes controls for repeated dialing, dual-SIM calls and automatic loudspeaker behavior.

The official screenshots show the redesigned Phone app surfaces and the preference pane. That makes the scope clear: Phonehub modifies several stages of a call, from selecting a number through the active call.

  • Look up unknown callers through multiple supported caller-ID sources, including global and regional services.
  • Switch the caller-ID engine during a call when another source is needed.
  • Choose a SIM before dialing on dual-SIM devices.
  • Automatically redial calls that are busy, unanswered or unavailable.
  • Use proximity-aware loudspeaker behavior while respecting Bluetooth and CarPlay.
  • Enable call-connect vibration and normalize country codes with local caching.

Compatibility and requirements

  • Havoc lists iOS 15.0 through iOS 17.0.
  • The current package listing identifies rootless support.
  • The public price was $3.99 when checked.
  • Caller-ID services may have their own availability, account, region or privacy conditions.
Important: Caller identification sends or compares phone-number information using the selected service. Review the provider, region and privacy implications before enabling a lookup engine, and do not assume every source works in every country.

Installation checklist

  1. Confirm iOS and jailbreak compatibility on the official Havoc page.
  2. Review which caller-ID sources are appropriate for your country before purchasing.
  3. Install Phonehub from Havoc and respring if requested.
  4. Configure one feature group at a time—caller ID, redial, SIM selection and speaker behavior.
  5. Place a non-urgent test call and confirm that emergency and carrier calling behavior remains normal.

Frequently asked questions

Is Phonehub only a caller-ID tweak?

No. Caller ID is a major feature, but the package also lists auto redial, dual-SIM selection and several in-call controls.

Can the lookup source be changed during a call?

The developer lists live in-call switching between supported caller-ID engines.

Does it support dual SIM?

Yes. A SIM picker before dialing is one of the listed features.

Which iOS versions are listed?

Havoc currently lists iOS 15.0 through iOS 17.0.
